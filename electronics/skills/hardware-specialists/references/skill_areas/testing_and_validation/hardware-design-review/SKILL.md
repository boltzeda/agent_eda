# Hardware Design Review Skill Router

Use this router for broad review requests. Select one primary review specialist
and at most two adjacent review specialists. Keep review independent from design
authoring unless the user separately requests changes.

## Available review specialists

- `chip-platform-and-board-co-design-review-engineer` — Chip Platform and Board Co-Design Review Engineer
- `communications-and-interface-design-review-engineer` — Communications and Interface Design Review Engineer
- `digital-analog-and-mixed-signal-design-review-engineer` — Digital Analog and Mixed-Signal Design Review Engineer
- `embedded-hardware-design-review-engineer` — Embedded Hardware Design Review Engineer
- `pcb-and-board-level-design-review-engineer` — PCB and Board-Level Design Review Engineer
- `power-and-power-electronics-design-review-engineer` — Power and Power Electronics Design Review Engineer
- `reliability-emc-and-safety-design-review-engineer` — Reliability EMC and Safety Design Review Engineer
- `testing-and-validation-review-engineer` — Testing and Validation Review Engineer

## Review contract

Confirm scope, artifact versions, lifecycle gate, interfaces, environment, and
applicable standards. Report each finding with severity, location, evidence,
risk mechanism, impact, recommended change, owner, and closure evidence. Use
Blocker, Major, Minor, or Question and finish with HOLD, CONDITIONAL, or READY.
